Digits to the right of the decimal point represent fractional tenths, hundredths, thousandths:

Rule: Pad with trailing zeros to make both numbers the same length before comparing! E.g., compare 0.450 to 0.405.

Percent means per 100:

  • % to Decimal: Divide by 100 (move decimal 2 places LEFT).
  • Decimal to %: Multiply by 100% (move decimal 2 places RIGHT).
0.4% = 0.4 ÷ 100 = 0.004
0.085 × 100% = 8.5% (Not 85%!)
